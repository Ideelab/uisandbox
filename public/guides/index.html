Guides

UISandbox opens your running app in your browser with every colour, font, radius and spacing as a knob. These pages answer the questions people bring to it.

  • Try a new design on the app you are runningTest a new look on your running app: one line opens your localhost app live in the browser, every colour, font and spacing a knob, then a patch.
  • Design on the app itself, not on a canvasTry colour, type, corners and spacing on your running app in the browser, with its real data and every screen, not on a canvas mock-up. Keep it as code.
  • Why your app looks AI-generatedThe generic AI look is measurable: Inter or Geist, an indigo brand, gradients and frosted glass. See which tells are on your screen and which a knob fixes.
  • Try designs from your agent: the UISandbox MCP serverAn MCP server for Claude Code, Cursor and Codex: it opens your running app in a design sandbox, waits while you turn the knobs, and returns a patch.
