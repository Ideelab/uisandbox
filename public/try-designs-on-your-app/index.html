Try a new design on the app you are running

Run one line next to your dev server and your app opens in UISandbox, live: every screen, hot reload kept. Turn its colour, type, shape and space, and every place a value is used follows. Keep what you like as a patch for your source, or close the tab and nothing happened.

Open the editorHow it works

One line, next to your dev server

npx uisandbox-mcp@latest open

It looks for a dev server on localhost and opens it in your own browser. When more than one runs, name it: npx uisandbox-mcp@latest open http://localhost:3000. With no dev server running, it opens the build in the current folder instead. Measured live on Vite with React and on Next.js 15 (App Router), each with its own hot reload. Your agent can do the same for you: see the MCP server.

What you can try

Press 6 over the screen and you see your own screen six ways, side by side: Classic, Compact, Soft, Sharp, Airy and Bold. They differ in corners, spacing, depth and type and never in colour, so you compare the style and not the paint. Pick one, then tune it. Colour, Type, Shape and Space come first; text size, line height, letter spacing, weight, border width, elevation, motion and grey tint are under More. Every dial has ×1 = as in your code at its centre, and holding Space shows your original.

It is your app, not a mock-up of it

Every colour, size, radius, font and shadow literal in your CSS becomes a variable holding the very same value, so the page renders exactly as it did. A colour used 144 times is one knob, and turning it moves all 144. Check 1:1 compares the computed styles of every element against the untouched page and names anything that differs. The reach meter says how much of what you see the knobs touch; images, canvas and video lie outside it.

What you take home

Copy for your AI puts the design system and how to apply it in one paste, for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Lovable or v0. On your own app you also get a patch for your source files, or tokens for CSS, Tailwind, shadcn, Swift and Android. With the MCP server running, Send to your agent hands it straight back, and the agent shows you the patch before it changes a file.

Questions

Does my code change while I try things?

No. The sandbox works on what your dev server serves. Your files change only when you or your agent apply the patch.

Does anything leave my machine?

No. The sandbox runs from 127.0.0.1 and renders in your browser. There is no account, no cookies and no analytics.

Which apps work?

Anything a browser renders from HTML and CSS. Measured live so far: Vite with React, and Next.js 15. It cannot render iOS or Android projects (it exports Swift and Android constants instead) or WordPress themes, and it says so at the door.

My app is on staging, or behind a login.

Drag Take this screen along to your bookmarks bar and click it on any screen your browser shows, a Lovable or v0 preview included. A zip, a folder or a public GitHub or GitLab repo work too.
