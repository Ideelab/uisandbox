UISandbox

Play with your app's design, right in the browser.

Start from a kit or your own app, turn the knobs, export the code.

Every colour, font, radius and spacing in an app's CSS becomes a knob, and the real app follows 1:1. Start from one of eight UI kits (shadcn/ui, Tailwind CSS, daisyUI, Bootstrap, Material 3, Radix Themes, Mantine, Ant Design) or bring your own build, a public repo or your running app. What you keep comes out as a design system and a skill for your agent, a patch, or design tokens.
